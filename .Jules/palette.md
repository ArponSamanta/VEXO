## 2026-11-02 - Accessible Hover & Keyboard Quick Actions on Product Cards
**Learning:** In e-commerce product cards where action buttons (e.g. Add to Cart, Wishlist) are hidden by default and revealed on hover over the product image, nesting `<button>` elements inside an `<a>` or `<Link>` tag breaks HTML semantics and causes keyboard trap/accessibility issues. Additionally, hover-only controls are inaccessible to keyboard users unless `group-focus-within` transitions are applied.
**Action:** Extract the image link to be a sibling of the quick action buttons within a shared `relative` image container, set `tabIndex={-1}` and `aria-hidden="true"` on the redundant image link when the title is linked, and reveal buttons and gradient overlay with `group-focus-within:opacity-100 group-focus-within:translate-y-0`.
