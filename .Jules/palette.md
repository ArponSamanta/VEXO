## 2026-07-27 - Accessible Product Grids and Star Ratings
**Learning:** In highly interactive dark-themed interfaces like Vexo, product card lists can easily lead to screen reader fatigue and focus trapping. First, having both the product image and product name link to the exact same route causes double tab stops and repetitive text-to-speech feedback. Setting `tabIndex={-1}` and `aria-hidden="true"` on the image-only link while keeping the text link focusable keeps focus flow exceptionally clean. Second, raw list of 5 individual star icons are extremely noisy on screen readers (e.g. reading "star, star, star..." instead of "4 out of 5 stars"). Standardizing star rating containers with `role="img"` and a descriptive `aria-label` while setting `aria-hidden="true"` on inner icons creates an optimal, concise announcement.
**Action:** Always combine multi-link card layouts into a single screen-readable text link and group ratings using a clean wrapper with `role="img"` and descriptive `aria-label`.
